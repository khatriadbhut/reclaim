What we collect
Websites you visit — domain, time spent, and category
e.g. amazon.in · shopping · 4 min
Page signals — brands, products, prices, search queries, scroll depth
e.g. Apple iPhone 15 Pro · intent score 8 · ₹134,900
Demographics you provide — age range, gender, occupation
Approximate location — city-level, from GPS if granted or your IP address if not
never street-level · never shared raw
How it's used
Your data is anonymized and grouped into audience segments
Segments are sold to companies for market research and ad targeting
Your real name, email, or raw IP is never sold or stored
Meta, Google, and Instagram do all of the above — silently, without paying you a cent. Reclaim shows you exactly what's collected and gives you a cut of every sale.
